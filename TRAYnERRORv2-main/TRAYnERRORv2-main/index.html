1. Seating & Queues

waiting_parties() returns guests at the entrance. free_tables() returns empty tables. Match small groups (1-2) to small tables, larger (3-4) to long tables.

seat(party, table)
2. Orders & Kitchen

send_to_kitchen(order)

📖 Tray & Error Handbook

1. Terminal Basics

Loops & Waiting

Scripts repeat with while True:. Indent the body by 4 spaces. Add wait(1) so the loop does not spin. A runaway loop is stopped by the watchdog.

while True:
    wait(1)
Errors & Storage

Errors appear in the terminal with a line number. Each terminal has a line budget; blanks and # comments are free. Buy RAM Expansion in the Shop for +15 lines.

2. Waiter Robot

Seating

waiting_parties() lists guests at the door. free_tables() lists empty tables. Match party size to seats, then:

seat(party, table)
Orders & Delivery

take_order(table) after 3s. Send it with send_to_kitchen(order). Take plated dishes from ready_dishes(), lock one with claim(dish), then deliver(dish, table).

Upkeep

Watch battery() and call go_charge() before it hits zero. Moving wears the robot down; check health() and call repair() at the charger ($15 day / $5 night) when it drops below 20%, or movement slows by 40%.

if health() < 20:
    repair()

3. Chef & Special Requests

Cooking

Read tickets with tickets(), take ingredients with grab("ingredients", 1), cook, then plate(ticket).

Notes Are Mandatory

A ticket may carry a note such as "no onions". Call handle_note(ticket) before plating. If you ignore it, the customer complains and the tip is lost.

handle_note(ticket)
cook(4)
plate(ticket)

4. Firmware & Fleet

Firmware Commands

nearest_table(status) finds the closest "free", "order" or "served" table. order_stock(qty) buys stock for $2 per portion. Both are locked until bought in the Shop.

Two Waiters

Both waiters share the counter. claim(dish) returns True for exactly one robot, so two scripts never grab the same plate.

5. Reservations, Spills & Refunds

Table Reservations

Guests book ahead! is_reserved(table) checks bookings, and reservations() returns arrival times. free_tables() hides reserved tables by default. Seating the VIP at their reserved table pays a +$15 VIP bonus!

if is_reserved(table):
    continue # Hold table for VIP
Floor Spills & Obstacles

Accidental spills appear on the floor. Robots route around them. Stepping on a puddle causes slipping and durability damage! Find puddles with spills() and mop them up with:

spls = spills()
if len(spls) > 0:
    clean(spls[0])
Refund System

Careless service costs money. Delayed food walkouts, missed special notes, or stolen reservations automatically issue cash refunds to customers.
